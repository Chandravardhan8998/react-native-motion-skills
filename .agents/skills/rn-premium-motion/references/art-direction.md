# Premium motion art direction without copying a brand

Three selectable directions (choose to match the **existing** product):

- **Quiet luxury**: deliberate typography, restrained easing, depth, calm surfaces, single accent and soft navigation continuity.
- **Playful expressive**: elastic spring on safe actions, warm status illustration, tiny confetti bursts on genuine success, tactile gestures.
- **Precision utility**: near-instant feedback, confident transitions, clear value changes, minimal decorative effects, informative motion.

Motion hierarchy: **state clarity > speed > focus > delight**. A screen can have one hero motion, 2–3 supportive micro-interactions, and zero background loops.

Signature opportunities: 3D product/card surfaces; status transitions; depth-based hero navigation; kinetic metric; animated chart; reveal-on-scroll; contextual overlay; guided onboarding; tactile sliders; empty-to-populated transitions.

Do not clone an existing company's copyrighted artwork, brand mark, screenshots or distinctive proprietary assets. Build on project colors/fonts and customer audience.
